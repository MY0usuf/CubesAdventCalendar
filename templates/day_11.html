Flashback to Where It All Began

It feels like just yesterday when our story started to unfold. Let me take you back to those moments that shaped everything, from the first messages to the unforgettable first meet.

The Instagram Slide

It all began with an innocent Insta message. Which i ghosted your bro for like 2 hrs, but it was the start of something I never imagined would turn into this beautiful journey.

Looking at Each Other in the Resort

Then came that moment at the resort, when our eyes met, and everything seemed to pause for a second. It was like the universe gave us a little nudge, letting us know we were meant to share this moment. Neither of us said much, but that look said everything.

The Rizz on WhatsApp

And then there was the *rizz*—you know, that smooth flow on WhatsApp. It was a mix of awkwardness and excitement, but it was all part of the magic. Every conversation brought us closer, and I couldn't get enough of talking to you. You made me laugh, and I couldn't stop smiling.

The Twinning Moments

Who could forget the first time we twinned on stage? It was accidental wink wink, but somehow it felt like destiny was playing its part. The universe was just showing off, making sure we matched not just in style but in heart.

The Accidental Twinning the Next Day

The next day, we accidentally twinned again, and it felt like fate was telling us we were meant to be. It was so funny that we both showed up in matching outfits without even planning it. That moment sealed it—I knew something special was happening. And now we just twin every single time like its nothing.

The "I Should Have Met You" Moment

I remember thinking, “I should have met you so much sooner.” But I guess we were meant to meet at the perfect time. I’ve never believed in fate, but this... this felt different. We were both ready to write our own story.

The Salam Moment

Do you remember the first time I said, “Salam, this is Muhammad Yousuf”? It was so formal, and yet so real. It was the start of something amazing, and in that moment, I knew I wanted to be more than just your friend. But then... I ghosted your brother for a bit. 😂 I had no idea how to respond, but I guess that’s part of the awkwardness of starting something new.

The First Meet

The day we met in person, I remember everything. I was so nervous, but I tried to play it cool. And then, there was me, eating like I hadn’t seen food in days. Your laugh, and I couldn’t stop myself from blushing. It wasn’t perfect, but it was our perfect beginning.

Every Moment Led to This

And now, here we are. Looking back at these moments brings me so much happiness, and I wouldn’t change a thing. Every laugh, every awkward moment, and every little memory has brought us here—and it’s been worth every second.
