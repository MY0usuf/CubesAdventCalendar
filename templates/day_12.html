Our Eid, the Way I Imagined It 🌙

This Eid, there was something missing. It was us. Side by side. Twinning. Laughing at silly things.

I wanted so badly for us to have that memory—so I made it. A moment we didn’t get to live, but one I dreamed of all the same.

I know we couldn’t be in the same room that day... but I wanted to show you how it looked in my heart.

You looked beautiful as always. I looked at peace. And we matched—like we always do in our own quiet elegant way. We should consider making a book of all the times we twinned and perfectly I have to add.

What should we name it? Art of Twinning - wink wink😉

Eid Mubarak, love.
From your far-away-but-always-there Sufii 🌙✨
