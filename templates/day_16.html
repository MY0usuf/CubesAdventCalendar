7 Wishes for You

My love, as the countdown continues, I wanted to share with you seven wishes, each one filled with love and hope for us, now and forever.

  • ✨1. I wish for your happiness, today and always, for you deserve nothing less than joy in every moment.
  • ✨2. I wish for our love to grow deeper every day, evolving into something even more beautiful than it is now.
  • ✨3. I wish for countless more memories, the kind that will make us smile every time we think of them.
  • ✨4. I wish for our future together to be full of laughter, adventure, and dreams we can make come true.
  • ✨5. I wish for us to always find our way back to each other, no matter the distance or time that separates us.
  • ✨6. I wish for your heart to always be at peace, knowing that you are my everything, today and forever.
  • ✨7. I wish for forever with you. No matter where life takes us, no matter what comes our way, my biggest wish is that we always have us. 7 days until you steal the spotlight, and I get to celebrate you the most precious gift life has ever given me.

Until then, know this: I am yours, completely, madly, endlessly. And I wouldn’t want it any other way. Happy almost-birthday week my love. Forever yours, Your biggest admirer, your always and forever. I can’t wait to make these wishes come true with you, my love. ❤️
