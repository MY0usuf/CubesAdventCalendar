5 Days Until You!

Good morning, my love! Another sunrise, another day closer to celebrating you. And guess what? We’re down to just one hand’s count—5 fingers, 5 days—until the world gets to honor the day you were born, the day my heart’s favorite person arrived on this earth.

If distance had a voice, it would hum the sweetest melody of anticipation, carrying my love to you with every sunrise. If time had wings, I’d clip them just to make these moments last a little longer—so I could cherish every heartbeat leading up to your special day. For now, I send you my love wrapped in the morning light, counting down not just days, but heartbeats until I can celebrate you properly.

  • 5... like the fingers on my hand, wishing they could hold yours right now.
  • 4... like the seasons we've shared, each one blossoming with memories that make my heart bloom.
  • 3... like the words I’ll say to you on your birthday, all wrapped up in love: *I love you*.
  • 2... like the nights we’ve stayed awake, talking about our dreams and our future together.
  • 1... like the moment I get to say: *Happy birthday, my love.*

Have a beautiful day, my love. The countdown continues, but my love for you is timeless. ❤️
