Shhh... This Day is Ours 🤫💌

Nine months ago, on this exact day, something quietly shifted. No grand gestures, no announcements — just a feeling that settled deep in my heart:

“It’s her. It’s always been her.”

Not everyone knows what 07/07 means.
But we do.

Maybe the world celebrates us on other days — but my heart always goes back to this one.
07/07. Just you & me.
