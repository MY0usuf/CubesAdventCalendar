4 Silent Gifts I Send You 🎁

Today, I’m sending you four gifts. They are gifts you can’t unwrap, but they are the most meaningful to me. These are the things that don’t need words to be understood. I hope you can feel them, because they’re all about you. 💖

  • 🎁 *Support*: The promise to always be there for you, through all the highs and lows of life.*
  • 🎁 *Patience*: My patience with you, knowing that no matter the pace, we’ll always walk through this journey together.*
  • 🎁 *Memories*: Every memory we make, every laugh, every shared moment — they stay with me forever.*
  • 🎁 *Trust*: The unwavering trust I have in you, knowing that our bond is built on understanding and love.*

These may not be the kind of gifts you can hold in your hands, but they are the ones that matter the most to me. They represent the deep, silent love I have for you. 💖

I hope you can feel these gifts, because they come straight from my heart. 💖
